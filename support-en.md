## Quick answers

**How do I get into the parent zone?**
Tap the small lock icon in the top right of the Home screen, then enter the PIN you set during onboarding.

**I forgot the parent PIN. What now?**
At the moment the only way to reset the PIN is to uninstall and reinstall the app. We're working on a less drastic reset option for a future update.

**How do I add another child?**
Parent Zone → Settings → Children → "Add another child." You can have up to 3 child profiles in one app, each with their own stars, streaks, and tasks.

**How do I change the language?**
Parent Zone → Settings → Language. Sparky supports English and Bulgarian.

**Why isn't the focus timer notification firing?**
Make sure notifications are enabled for Sparky in iOS Settings → Notifications → Sparky Kids. The "task complete" sound is scheduled when you start the timer and fires when it reaches zero — even if the app is in the background.

**Do you collect any data about my child?**
No. Sparky is fully offline. See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-en.md) for the full details.

**Is there an Android version?**
We're working on it. The iOS version is our first release.
